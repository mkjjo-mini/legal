About the service

Fitboard helps people rediscover the clothes they already own. Users save full-look outfit photos, the app identifies the garments in each photo and builds a personal wardrobe, then suggests new combinations from items the user already has.

핏보드는 사용자가 이미 가진 옷을 다시 발견하게 돕는 서비스입니다. 풀 착장 코디 사진을 저장하면 사진 속 아이템을 인식해 개인 옷장을 만들고, 보유한 옷으로 만들 수 있는 새로운 조합을 제안합니다.

Platform

Apps in Toss — a mini-app platform inside Toss, one of Korea's largest financial super-apps. Mini-apps run without installation and are discoverable to Toss users through in-app search and rankings.

앱인토스 — 토스 앱 안에서 설치 없이 실행되는 미니앱 플랫폼입니다.

Audience

  • Women in their 20s–40s living in Korea
  • Interested in minimal, neutral-toned everyday styling
  • High intent: users arrive with a specific question — "what do I wear today" or "what should I buy next"

How products are featured

Fitboard surfaces retail products in the context of the user's own wardrobe. A product is shown together with outfit combinations built from items the user already owns, and links out to the retailer's official store.

  • Product images are displayed from the retailer's own image URLs — not copied, cached, resized or modified
  • Brand names appear as plain text only. No brand logos or wordmark images are used
  • Every outbound link carries a disclosure that Fitboard is not the seller
  • Fitboard does not process orders, payments or shipping

상품은 사용자의 옷장 아이템과 함께 조합으로 제시되며, 구매는 각 브랜드 공식몰에서 이루어집니다. 상품 이미지는 원본 URL로 표시만 하고 복사·캐시·수정하지 않습니다.
