1. What we collect

IMNACO805 (아임나코805) ("we") is the controller of your personal data for Toile. We collect only what we need to run the Service.

[Sign-in]
· Your email address (if you sign in with email)
· Your Apple or Google account identifier and email (if you sign in with Apple or Google)

[What you save]
· Outfit and clothing photos, and the details we recognize in them or you enter (clothing attributes such as category and color, outfit tags such as style, occasion and season, and item names)
· The original web address, if you saved a photo from one
· Wear logs, saved combinations and AI outfit images
· The city you choose for weather (we never collect your device location)

[Public posts and reports]
· Your public profile name, whether you are an adult, and whether you declared a photo as your own
· Reports you make or receive and how they were handled, the users you have blocked, and the outfits and users you have hidden

[Creator and business applications — only if you apply]
· Creator application: your introduction, and optionally your channel address and follower count
· Business application: business name, business registration number, representative's name, store address, contact email, and optionally a phone number
· The type and status of your application (in review, approved, returned), when you applied and when we decided, and notes our team makes while reviewing it
· If approved, your account type and when it was approved
· If you enter another person's details, such as a representative or contact person, please tell them first.

[Collected automatically]
· Usage events (which screens and features you use), device and OS type, app language, and timestamps
· Your reactions in the app (saving, liking, hiding, wearing) and viewing records (which outfits were shown to you, which you opened, and how long you stayed)
· We link usage records to a member ID, not your email.
· We never record web addresses, photo paths, email addresses or item names in usage events, and we do not use session recording.

Before a photo leaves your device, we remove its embedded metadata (EXIF), including location.

2. Why we use it, and our legal bases

For users in the EEA and UK, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR:

· Contract — to create your account, store your photos, recognize clothing, and provide your wardrobe, combinations, wear log, weather outfits, public posts and personalized recommendations (choosing and ordering the outfits recommended in Discover, based on what you save and how you use the app); and, if you apply for a creator or business account, to review your application at your request and run that account type
· Legitimate interests — to keep the Service safe (checking public photos for objectionable content, handling reports, preventing abuse) and to improve it with usage statistics linked to a member ID rather than your name or email. You can object at any time (see section 6).
· Legal obligation — to respond to valid legal requests and keep records required by law
· Consent — to make an outfit public. You can withdraw consent by making it private again.

3. How long we keep it

· When you delete your account, we delete your account, photos, wardrobe, combinations, wear log and public outfits without delay. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
· When you delete a photo or outfit, we delete it right away.
· Copies that other users saved from an outfit you made public stay in their libraries. We delete them when that user deletes them, or within 10 days of your asking us to. You can ask even after deleting your account.
· So that we can act on a request made after you delete your account, we keep the ID numbers of outfits you had made public and a one-way hash of your email. We delete these once no copies of those outfits remain, and use them only to verify takedown requests.
· Creator and business application details are kept for as long as you hold that account type. If we return an application, we delete it 1 year after the decision (a period we chose, to spot repeated applications). If you delete your account, they are deleted then.
· Records of how rights complaints were handled are kept for 3 years after the complaint is closed, to deal with disputes. This period is our own choice, not a statutory one.
· Usage records (usage statistics, and what you viewed and reacted to) are deleted after 1 year, or kept only as aggregate numbers that cannot identify you. If you delete your account, they are deleted then.
· We keep data longer only where the law requires it.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act). We do not give it to third parties, except where required by law.

If you make an outfit public, that photo and your public profile name are visible to other users, and a copy stays in the library of anyone who saves it. This is your choice.

Shopping links take you to retailer and affiliate-network sites (such as Rakuten). Their own privacy policies apply there.

6. Your rights

· In the app, you can view, edit and delete your photos, items and records yourself, make public outfits private, and delete your account. Copies other users saved from your public outfits are not removed automatically — email us and we will delete them (section 3).
· Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to processing (including analytics based on legitimate interests), and to withdraw consent.
· California residents have the right to know, delete and correct their personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information.
· To exercise a right, email us at the address below. We respond within the time required by law (one month in the EEA and UK). We will not charge you.
· In the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Toile is not for children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA and UK, or the age of digital consent if lower; under 14 in Korea). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn we have, we delete it immediately.

8. Security

· Saved photos are kept in private storage that only the person who saved them can access. When another user saves your public outfit, their copy sits in their private storage.
· Public outfits are copied to separate public storage, apart from private storage.
· All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
· Usage events contain nothing that directly identifies you.
